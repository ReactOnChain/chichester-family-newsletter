Chichester Family Newsletter

Coverage: 20 September - 11 October 2026 (plus a few book-ahead bits after that)

Hello,

Here are a few local things for babies through primary age around Chichester and the nearby villages over the next few weeks. I have checked the dates and prices on the organiser pages where I could.

Babies & under 2s

Friday 25 September - After Party at The Nest (CFT)

Ages 0-2, about 90 minutes. Live music and gentle sensory play from HurlyBurly. Late arrivals and come-and-go are fine. Brochure lists sessions at 11am and 2.30pm, so confirm times when you book.

£20 for one adult and one baby; extra adults £10.

Book After Party

Toddlers & preschool

Friday 25 September - Dinky Dragon's Toddler Club: Autumn Adventure

Ages 2-4 at Weald & Downland, Singleton (further afield). 10.30am-12 noon. Stories, songs, crafts and outdoor play with Knucker the dragon puppet.

£9.50 per child (adult free; extras £5). Must book, not sold on the day. Includes museum entry afterwards.

Book Dinky Dragons

Friday 16 October - Harbour Tots: Feathered Friends (book ahead)

Harbour Education Centre, Dell Quay, 10-11.30am. Outdoor toddler session on wetland birds; make a bird feeder. Dress for the weather.

Book via TicketSource from the Conservancy page. Listings put tickets around £7 for one adult and one child / £3 for extras. Confirm at checkout.

Harbour Tots info & booking

Primary

A Midsummer Night's Dream at CFT (runs to 17 October)

Family offer: half price for up to four under-16s with each full-price adult (not Saturday evenings).

Content includes strobe, lasers, haze and loud music, so better for older children who are happy with that. Running time still not published on the organiser page as of 19 September.

See Midsummer & family offer

All ages

Saturday 26 September - Midhurst Music and Movies on the Square

Further afield - Midhurst. Free outdoor event from 12 noon: Latin-inspired live music, kids' karaoke, face painting and a chance to try instruments; Pixar's Coco at 2pm; La La Land at 6pm.

Bring a camping chair or rug. Organised by Chichester District Council.

CDC What's On

Saturday-Sunday 26-27 September - Made by Hand at Weald & Downland

Further afield - Singleton. Heritage crafts weekend with live demonstrations, 10am-5pm. Included in daytime admission.

Peak online from £16.90 adult / £8.80 child 5-17 / under-5s free. Book online to save.

Saturday-Sunday 3-4 October - Michaelmas living history

Singleton. The Tudor Group run a traditional market around the square, 10am-5pm, included in museum admission. Woodland play area on site for a run-around.

Free anytime

Library rhyme & toddler sessions

Free drop-in (may fill). Chichester Rhyme Time and Toddler Time are at The Transformation Centre, 21 Orchard Street (PO19 1DD) while the library's first floor is closed. Rhyme Time Tuesdays 11.30am-12 noon; Toddler Time Wednesdays 11-11.30am.

Villages: Selsey Library Rhyme Time Tuesdays 10.30-11am; Witterings Thursdays 10.30-11am.

Southbourne Library is closed for works until Saturday 24 October. Rhyme Time moves to Southbourne Village Hall, Fridays 3-3.30pm.

CFT Pop-Up Family Fun

Free foyer play for ages 0-5 on Thursdays in term time and Saturdays 9am-12 noon (toys, colouring, fancy dress, books). Café next door; Oaklands Park outside.
